Title: TUB — Frequently Asked Questions (Cards & Accounts)

Q: How do I block a lost or stolen card?
A: You can block your card instantly in the TUB mobile app or by calling the
24/7 hotline. No credentials are needed.

Q: What is the TUB Premium account?
A: TUB Premium is a current account with free worldwide ATM withdrawals,
travel insurance and a priority support line, for a EUR 9.90 monthly fee
(waived with EUR 2,000 monthly incoming payments).

Q: How do I upgrade to TUB Premium?
A: Existing clients can upgrade from TUB Standard directly in the mobile app.
