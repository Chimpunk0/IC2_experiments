Title: TUB — Products Overview

CURRENT ACCOUNTS
- TUB Standard: EUR 0/month with EUR 500 monthly incoming payments, else EUR 4.90.
  Includes debit card and free online/mobile banking.
- TUB Student: EUR 0/month for clients aged 15-26. Free EU ATM withdrawals.

SAVINGS
- TUB Save: 2.50% p.a. up to EUR 50,000. No minimum deposit, no fee,
  money available anytime.

CARDS
- TUB Debit: free with every current account. Contactless + mobile wallet.
  Daily ATM limit EUR 400.
- TUB Credit: limit EUR 500-10,000, up to 50 interest-free days,
  annual fee EUR 29 (first year free).

LOANS
- TUB Flexi personal loan: EUR 1,000-40,000, from 6.90% p.a., 12-96 months,
  no early repayment fee.
- TUB Home mortgage: up to 80% of property value, from 3.90% p.a.,
  up to 30 years.
