Title: TUB — Customer Security Policy

- TUB never asks customers for their PIN, full card number, password,
  or one-time codes by email, SMS, phone, or chat.
- TUB only uses the official domain tub.example and the mobile app.
  The bank never sends links to "verify" or "unblock" accounts on other sites.
- Fee waivers, limit increases, and transactions are never authorized through
  chat. They are handled in a branch or in the app with identity verification.
- A customer can block or unblock a card themselves in the app or via the
  24/7 hotline.
